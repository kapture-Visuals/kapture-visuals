What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They help websites function correctly, improve user experience, remember preferences, and provide information about website usage.

Cookies may be placed directly by Kapture Visuals (“first-party cookies”) or by trusted third-party service providers (“third-party cookies”).

Types of Cookies Used

a. Essential Cookies

These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the Site and cannot be disabled in our systems. They enable the website to save your privacy preferences and ensure the proper technical functioning of the features offered.

These cookies do not require your prior consent.

b. Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to measure the Site’s traffic and analyse how visitors use our pages to improve our content, services, and the Site’s performance. The data collected is generally aggregated and anonymized.

These cookies are only placed with your prior consent.

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner allows you to:

Your choice is stored and can be modified at any time through the cookie settings feature available on the website.
