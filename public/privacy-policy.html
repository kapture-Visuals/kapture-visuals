Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kapture Visuals (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you visit our website (https://kapturevisuals.netlify.app) or interact with us through our contact form.

We are committed to processing personal data transparently, securely, and in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation – GDPR) and all applicable Belgian and European data protection legislation.

This Privacy Policy applies to all services, forms, and interactions available through our website.

Kapture Visuals

Email: kapture.visuals.production@gmail.com

Country of Operation: Belgium

Personal Data Collected

Depending on your interaction with our website, we may collect different categories of personal data.

a. Information You Provide

When you contact us through our contact form, we may collect name, company name, email address, phone number (optional), service requested, and any information voluntarily included in your enquiry.

b. Technical and Usage Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect IP address, browser type and version, device information, operating system, pages visited, referring websites, website interaction data, cookie preferences, analytics information, and session and performance data.

Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

In accordance with Article 6(1) of the GDPR, we process personal data only for specific, explicit, and legitimate purposes.

a. Management of Contact Requests and Project Enquiries

On the basis of pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or Kapture Visuals’ legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in responding to enquiries received through the Website, Kapture Visuals may process your personal data in order to respond to your requests, contact you, follow up on communications, discuss potential projects or collaborations, and provide the information or services requested.

b. Client and Professional Relationship Management

On the basis of pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or Kapture Visuals’ legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in managing professional relationships, Kapture Visuals may process your personal data in order to communicate with you regarding our activities, manage ongoing projects, provide creative services, maintain professional exchanges, and organise potential collaborations or service engagements.

c. Website Security and Proper Functioning

On the basis of Kapture Visuals’ legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in ensuring the security, stability, and proper functioning of the Website, Kapture Visuals may process certain technical data in order to detect potential security incidents, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain website integrity, and ensure the overall security of the Website and its services.

d. Statistical Analysis and Website Improvement

Subject to your consent where required (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for analytics cookies and similar technologies, Kapture Visuals may process certain technical and usage data in order to measure website traffic, analyse visitor behaviour, improve user experience, optimise website performance, and enhance the content and services offered through the Website.

Contact Forms and Project Enquiries

Our website uses Netlify Forms to process contact form submissions.

When you submit a project enquiry, the information you provide is transmitted to and stored by Netlify on our behalf. We use this information solely to review enquiries, communicate with prospective clients, discuss projects and collaborations, and provide requested services.

We do not sell, rent, or share enquiry data for marketing purposes.

Google Analytics

Subject to your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website.

Google Analytics may collect information including pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, device and browser information, general geographic information, and website interaction data.

Google Analytics is activated only after consent has been provided.

Microsoft Clarity

Subject to your consent, we use Microsoft Clarity to analyse website usability and visitor interactions. Microsoft Clarity may collect information including click activity, scroll behaviour, navigation patterns, session interaction data, and device and browser information.

Microsoft Clarity may use cookies and similar technologies, including local storage, session storage, browser identifiers, and network requests.

The information collected is used exclusively for analytics, usability improvements, and website optimisation.

Additional information can be found at: https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Typical retention periods include:

  • Contact form enquiries: up to 3 years after the last meaningful interaction;
  • Cookie consent records: for the duration of the consent cookie;
  • Analytics data: according to the retention settings configured within the relevant analytics platform;
  • Legal and accounting records: as required by applicable law.

At the end of the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, disclosure, alteration, and misuse.

While we take reasonable steps to safeguard personal data, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
